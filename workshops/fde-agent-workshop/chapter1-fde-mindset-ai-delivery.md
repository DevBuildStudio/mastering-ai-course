# Chapter 1: The FDE Mindset in AI Delivery

## Why This Chapter Matters
Most AI projects fail between demo and deployment. FDEs close that gap by combining product thinking, systems thinking, and execution in real customer environments.

## Learning Objectives
- Define the scope of FDE work in AI programs.
- Distinguish prototype success from production success.
- Map technical decisions to business outcomes.

## Core Concepts
- Problem framing before model selection.
- Customer workflow analysis and integration points.
- Risk ownership across reliability, security, and trust.
- Delivery milestones: prototype, pilot, production.

## Practical Framework
Use the FDE decision lens for every feature:
1. User impact: What customer decision is improved?
2. Technical feasibility: Can the system run under constraints?
3. Operational readiness: Can we monitor and support it?
4. Business alignment: Does ROI justify complexity?

## Exercise
Pick one internal process and design an AI assistant for it.
- Define target users.
- Define failure costs.
- Define one launch metric and one rollback trigger.

## Output
A one-page FDE brief with scope, constraints, risks, and launch criteria.
